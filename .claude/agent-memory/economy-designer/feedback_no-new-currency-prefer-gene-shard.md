Product owner rejected my recommended option ("D" — a dedicated new currency
"Echo Fragment") for gating the Echo Box cosmetic sink, even though I flagged it
as economically cleaner. Decision (2026-09-07), verbatim: *"tiền mở unlock cần
rất ít Gene Shard. tuy nhiên cách làm của bạn là currency cũng hay nhưng chưa
phù hợp với playtest. Hiện cứ dùng Gene shard, sau này tôi sẽ làm cơ chế để vừa
dùng gene shard và currency mới."*

**Why:** Not a rejection of the idea itself — owner says a dedicated currency
"cũng hay" (is also good) but isn't validated by playtest yet. The idea is
deferred, not dead; owner intends to design a future hybrid mechanic that uses
BOTH Gene Shard and a new currency together.

**How to apply:** Do not propose adding a new currency as the primary/only fix
for a gating or sink problem unless asked. Default to reusing Gene Shard (the
sole soft currency in `design/gdd/economy-progression.md` §10.1) with threshold/
price tuning instead. If a new-currency idea is genuinely the best option,
present it as one of several options but expect Gene-Shard-only to be picked
unless the owner explicitly asks to revisit currency design. See
[[project-echo-box-daily-mission-2026-09]] for the concrete case this came from.
