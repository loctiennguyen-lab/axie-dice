# Quick bot sim, 12/10/2026 (RULES_VERSION godot-2026-10-12-f)

Measurement only, no rule/data file changed. Driver: `godot/tools/sim_quick.gd/.tscn` in worktree
`my-game-wt/SIM`, branch `sim/quick-1012` (not pushed). ~30 runs/s, headless.

## Method
- Bot = `RunBot` (tools/run_bot.gd): legal moves only, every dmg/poison/debuff face at the FIRST enemy, reward pick #0,
  first map branch, no shop buying, event option 0. Mode `short`, unranked, `newcomer=false` (so attrition and light
  A0 clock are ON). Ascension 0..10.
- Teams (7): default plant1/beast1/aqua1/reptile1/bug1 (N=500 per level) and 6 class-heavy teams (N=150 per level):
  plant-heavy, beast-heavy, aqua-heavy, reptile-heavy, bug-heavy, bird-heavy. These are class teams, NOT the 11 design
  archetypes (no archetype-targeted team builder exists in the bot).
- Same seeds across ascension levels (5000 + i*7919), so level-to-level steps are paired-ish.
- 95% CI = normal approx, shown as ±points.

## Bot cannot play / excluded
- Pacts (A6 First Pact, pact screens): bot never takes them, so the A6 pact effect is not measured (only HP/dmg part).
- Aid / Continue, Mission rules, shop buying, rerolls (bot never rerolls), heal/shield targeting smarts, A9 Hunted
  not verified as exercised. Rewards always #0, so no build is formed.
- Hence the bot is far weaker than any human profile. Mapping to casual/regular/heavy: NOT possible directly. Best
  single team (bug-heavy) is the nearest thing to "casual" in level (A0 53, A10 9), default team is below casual.

## 3. Shard per run (R)

| A0 | 299 | 409 | 572 |
| A3 | 236 | 362 | 573 |
| A6 | 192 | 323 | 571 |
| A10 | 116 | 240 | 592 |

- Winners always bank ~570 (about 5% above the older probe's 568); losers bank by depth (~270-350 at A0).
- Bot-level R at A0 is 300-410, under 450. Dominated by win rate: at 26% pooled win, R=409. Reweighting to a human
  40% win at A0 (0.4*572 + 0.6*354) gives about 441. At 60% (regular) about 480-500. So R≈450 looks right for A0
  casual/regular, but R falls with ascension: pooled bot R at A10 is 59% of A0, and a casual (8% win at A10 target)
  would bank roughly 0.08*590 + 0.92*~230 = ~260. Economy-v2 using a flat 450 over-pays high-ascension players by
  ~40% if they actually play at their target win rates. Flag to economy-designer.

## Design numbers that look off
1. Weakest-archetype A10 >=8%: bot fails on 5 of 7 teams (0-1.3%).
2. A6 and A9 steps are 1.1 and 1.6 pooled vs target 6-8; needs pact/Hunted-capable bot (sim F proper). A3 is fine.
3. Regular steps below the 2-point floor in 6 of 7 cases (low absolute base; not meaningful for the target table).
4. Class spread: reptile/bug much stronger than plant/aqua/beast under this bot.
5. Enrage: ablation delta not detectable; reach rates 4% / 31% / 65% at A0 / A4 / A9.
6. R flat 450 vs ascension-dependent R (above).

## Review (economy-designer, 13/10)
Checked the report against ascension-v2 §5.1/acceptance, economy-v2 (R=450, sensitivity 350/570) and monetization-v1 §C. Method skimmed only: sim_quick.gd takes a seed per run and calls `RunState.start_new_run`, and reads `RunState.shards_this_run`, so Shard is computed by the game itself. Looks fine. I did not re-run anything.

**What is solid**
- Default-team CIs are right (9.0% at N=500 gives ±2.5). Same seeds across levels make the A-to-A steps paired, so the pooled -7.0 at A3 is believable as "A3 is the one visible step".
- Winners bank ~570 at every level (572/573/571/592). That is the most reliable number in the report.
- Enrage reach rates (4% / 31% / 65%) are a usable upper bound, and the report labels them as one.

**What is not solid**
- "0.0±0.0" at A10 is a normal-approximation artefact. Use Wilson or the rule of three (0/500 means below ~0.6%).
- Pooled CIs treat 500 default + 6x150 as one random sample. It is a fixed mix of teams, so the CI says nothing about "the game". The pooled column is fine for shape, not for a ±3 acceptance check.
- Per-team steps (plant -13 at A3 but beast -4.6) are noise at N=150, as the report says. Do not read gates from single teams.
- The enrage ablation (-0.2 pts, CI ±1.4, base win rate 0.4% at A9) cannot detect anything. "Not measurable" is correct, but it also means monetization §C's clock has no balance evidence yet. Do not cite it as "clock is harmless".
- Gate check for A6/A9 is meaningless with this bot (no pacts, Hunted unverified). Do not mark ascension acceptance as passed or failed from this run.
- The A0 "43.7 spread" compares a mixed default team to a reptile-heavy team. That is not the archetype spread the 25-point cap is about.

**Class balance claim (reptile/bug 2-5x): treat as a bot artefact until proven otherwise.** The bot sends every dmg/poison/debuff to the first enemy and never rerolls. That is the ideal play for stacking poison and single-target debuffs, and it is the worst play for AoE, targeted heal/shield and reroll-reliant classes. No rewards are picked, so no relic-set forms, and the 25-point cap is a relic-set gate. Teams are class mixes, not the 11 archetypes. Real signal: only "the bot gets a lot out of poison/debuff stacking". Do not nerf or buff reptile/bug from this.

**R by ascension: direction right, implication overstated.** The mechanism is real (R is almost entirely win rate: 570 for a win, ~230-350 for a loss). But "flat 450 over-pays high A by ~40%" assumes a casual at 8% win at A10. Players at A10 are a selected group (they got there), so their win rate is much higher than 8%. At 50% win you get ~0.5x590 + 0.5x300 = ~445, which is about flat. The 40% number also uses bot loser banking, and humans lose deeper. Economy-v2 already carries the 350/570 sensitivity. The real finding is "R is a function of win rate, not of ascension", so the timeline sim should take R(win%, depth) instead of one constant. Also the bot buys nothing, while 450 is net of shop spend, so bot R is not directly comparable to the 450 target.

**Findings Liam should act on**
1. Do not change class/archetype balance from this report. Needs sim F with a bot that rerolls, targets AoE/heal sensibly, and picks rewards.
2. Ascension acceptance is not decided. Only A3 is confirmed as a gate. A6 and A9 need a pact- and Hunted-capable bot (confirm A9 Hunted is actually triggering).
3. Economy: keep R=450 at A0-A5 for now. Replace the single R by R(win%) in the timeline sim, using 350/570 as the bounds. Check the relation between ascension and win rate for real players before anyone says A10 over-pays.
4. Clock: ablation needs a stronger player (reptile/bug teams, or sim F bot) at N>=1000 per cell before the monetization §C values (start turn 10/8, light clock 14/+10%) are called tuned.
5. The A10 weakest-archetype 8% floor can only be judged on archetype teams with a real bot. The 0-1.3% here is a bot floor, not a design failure.

**Sim F must add:** the 11 archetypes at 300 seeds each, reward picking and relic-set formation, rerolls, smart targeting for AoE/heal/shield, pacts (A6/A10) and Hunted, shop buying (so R is net like the 450), Wilson CIs, per-team results reported with the paired-seed difference rather than levels, and clock ablation on teams winning 30%+.
