## Review (narrative-director)

Rules applied: SET in caps everywhere (glossary form), Mana capitalised, no "dmg" where there is room, tradeoff cards show the benefit, no jargon the new player will not know. 24 lines changed (old → new, reason):

Cores (20)
- c_meme_copycat: "Weak die copies the Set" → "Weak die copies the SET". Keyword form.
- c_hunters_reward: "Set reroll refunds Mana" → "SET reroll refunds Mana". Keyword form.
- c_prime_chain: "Shield/mana: next hit +3" → "Shield/Mana: next hit +3". Keyword form.
- c_cracked_glass: "Cracked dice pierce all" → "Cracked die hits all". "Pierce all" read as armor-piercing; the Core sprays shards at every enemy.
- c_field_medic: "Support faces +3" → "Non-damage faces +3". Wrong: it buffs heal, shield, poison, mana and debuff faces, and poison is not support.
- c_prism: "4 types: Shield & Mana" → "Mix 4 faces: Shield+Mana". "4 types" of what was unclear.
- c_support_hand: "Set: support faces +2" → "SET: support faces +2". Keyword form.
- c_wicked_tally: "Kills make finisher +1" → "Finisher +1 per kill". Reads plainly and uses the same "Finisher" word as Chimera Hunt.
- c_magma_seed: "Held twice: AoE blast" → "Held 2 turns: hits all". "AoE" is jargon, and "twice" hid that it means two turns in a row.
- c_mana_pool: "Mana faces +1, save Mana" → "Unspent Mana earns more". "Save Mana" read like an order; the Core's bonus is the interest on unspent Mana.
- c_thorn_pact: "Rerolls pay HP, not Mana" → "Rerolls cost no Mana". Tradeoff card, so the benefit goes in the headline and the HP cost in the description.
- c_gatekeeper: "Front hit: thorns + dmg" → "Front hit: Thorns, buff". "dmg" removed and Thorns capitalised; the damage bonus goes to your team and the old line did not say so.
- c_melting_pot: "Mixed team: Sets +0.25 M" → "Mixed team: SET +0.25 M". Keyword form, same length.
- c_squad_up: "Class neighbours: dmg +2" → "Class pairs: damage +2". "dmg" was avoidable.
- c_living_wall: "Slot 1 tank +12 HP" → "One front Axie +12 HP". "Slot 1" and "tank" mean nothing to a new player, and "one front Axie" says the front row shrinks to one.
- c_chimera_hunt: "Finish kills earn Cores" → "Finisher kills win Cores". "Finish kills" is not a phrase; now matches Wicked Tally.
- c_meme_nametag: "Named enemy gives Mana" → "Kill named foe: +1 Mana". The old line did not say you have to kill it or how much Mana.
- c_loop: "Cantrips reroll, used 2x" → "Reroll faces used twice". "Cantrip" is jargon the brief rules out.
- c_sacrifice: "Four Axies +2, +1 reroll" → "+2 faces, +1 free reroll". The old line did not say what gets +2 or that the reroll is free.
- c_verdict: "Execute foes under 20%" → "Foes under 20% HP die". "Execute" is a hard word for a new player and the line left out HP.

Run Rules (4)
- rule_high_stakes: "Gamble rerolls, no Mana" → "Free Gamble rerolls". The old line could be read as Gambles costing nothing in risk.
- rule_rising_tide: "Front -25% or back +2" → "Row buffed each fight". The old line did not say front takes 25% less damage, or that the buff alternates by fight.
- rule_set_is_king: "Set dice +2, others -2" → "Dice in a SET get +2". Benefit-first for a tradeoff rule, and keyword form.
- rule_tundra: "Hold 2 dice, held +2" → "Hold 2 dice, Held +2". Keyword form.
