Placement rules — nothing outside the land
1 · The land is one diamond plateau centred on the Portal (960, 590): a point is on land when |dx|/900 + |dy|/450 ≤ 1. Cliff band below the front edges. 2 · Every object's base point must be ≤ 0.92 (blue dashed line) so models never hang over the cliff lip. 3 · Buildings sit on the 0.67 lattice ring around the Portal (the Land.gd 3×3 lattice). Collision is tested on the ground footprint (model width × 48 px at the base) + 32 px. Tall models may rise above the back edge into the sky, which is normal in perspective; the Leaderboard stands behind the Portal this way (Land.gd), its footprint is clear of the Portal's. Decor keeps out of the whole model box + 32 px so nothing hides behind a building. 4 · Walking lanes run from the hub in front of the Portal to each building's front, 56 px wide, kept free of decor. 5 · Tall trees (T) only on the back rim, 0.76–0.92, ≥ 90 px apart, skipping the arc behind the Leaderboard. They stand behind buildings as a backdrop: a tree only needs its base clear of building footprints + 32 px and its whole model clear of name plates. The front rim gets only shrubs (S) and rocks (R) so nothing hides a building or a name plate. 6 · Inner shrubs and rocks: 0.35–0.85, ≥ 64 px apart, split evenly between the back and front halves, never in a keep-out, a lane, a name plate or the player's Axie spot. Placement is seeded per Land so it never reshuffles between visits.
Decor here is generated with these rules (seed 7), so the mockup is a real test of them. Biome ground, cliff and sky colours and the actual tree/shrub/rock meshes come from Land.gd BIOMES. Gallery uses a stand-in mesh (Armorsmith) in code until the real model is named.
